# My Band – APK releases

Signed APK builds of **My Band** (`io.github.dmitryzemlyuk.band`), a lightweight Android companion app
for Mi Band 7, Amazfit Bip Max and Mi Body Composition Scale 2 that works with your existing Zepp Life / Zepp account.
This repository holds **APK builds only**, no sources.

- **Install:** download the newest `apk/band-<version>.apk` and open it on the phone. Latest: see [`latest.json`](latest.json).
- **Updates:** the app checks `latest.json` here about once a day and offers to update through the Android system installer. Updates install over the previous version and keep all data (same signing key).
- No ads, analytics or third-party SDKs. Ukrainian, Russian and English.
- Requires Android 10 or newer.
