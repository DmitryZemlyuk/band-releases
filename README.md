# My Band – releases

Signed APK builds of **My Band** (`io.github.dmitryzemlyuk.band`), a lightweight Android companion app
for Mi Band 7, Amazfit Bip Max and Mi Body Composition Scale 2 that works with your existing Zepp Life / Zepp account.

- No ads, analytics or third-party SDKs
- Ukrainian, Russian and English
- The app checks this repository once a day and installs updates through the Android system installer

Download the APK from the latest release.
